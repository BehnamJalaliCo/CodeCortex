# Code of Conduct

CodeCortex is committed to a professional, welcoming, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior quickly.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible-disclosure processes.
- Give credit for contributions and avoid misrepresenting another person's work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, doxxing, credential exposure, sexualized harassment, or retaliation against good-faith reporters is not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its community. Serious security matters should be reported privately using the process in `SECURITY.md`.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and focused on restoring a safe and productive environment.
